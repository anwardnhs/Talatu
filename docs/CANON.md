## Identity and geography

- Registered name: **Talatu Plc**; listing: **NGX: TALATU**.
- Nigerian media and entertainment group, founded in **1962** as Jaridah Media Company in **Kaduna**. Do not describe Lagos as the original founding location.
- Founder: Faisal Alhassan, CFR. Founding editor: Mukhtar Bello, CFR.
- Corporate headquarters: **Talatu House, Ikoyi, Lagos**.
- Operational headquarters: **Southstone Campus, Lekki–Epe Expressway, Lagos**; newsroom, studios, post-production and animation hub.
- Eight operating markets: Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia and Ghana. **Egypt is an associate market**, not a ninth wholly operated market.
- Financial year ends 31 December. Financial figures below refer to FY2025, ended 31 December 2025.

## Leadership and governance

**Anwar Alhassan is Executive Chairman. David Saro is CEO.** The chairman's remit is capital allocation, shareholder relations, Group strategy, intellectual property and rights stewardship. Operating decisions belong to the CEO; editorial decisions belong to editors under Editorial Committee oversight. Do not describe the chairman as running daily operations or directing newsroom coverage.

The executive team is David Saro (CEO), Femi Ayodeji (CFO), Aliyu Mania (President & COO), Ovie Ejiro (Chief Content Officer), Felix Onwudiwe (Chief Product Officer), Emmanuel Tabi (Chief Technology Officer), Tomiye Alagoa (Chief Commercial Officer), Temiloluwa Adebayo (Chief People Officer), Murtala Sule (General Counsel & Company Secretary), and Naledi Dlamini (Chief Corporate Affairs Officer).

The 11-member Board has four executive directors, five independent directors and two non-executive directors. The five standing committees are Audit, Risk, Governance & Nomination, Remuneration and Editorial.

Independent directors: Dr. Bukola Adewale (Lead Independent Director, Audit chair), Goni Bukar (Governance & Nomination chair), Obinna Ezeh (Risk chair), Hadiza Yerima (Remuneration chair), Angela Nwana (Editorial chair). Non-executive directors: Amira Alhassan (Alhassan Family Office representative), Emeka Anyanwu (minority shareholders' representative). Executive directors: Anwar Alhassan, David Saro, Femi Ayodeji, Aliyu Mania.

The family vehicles do not vote on CEO appointments, which are reserved to independent directors under the shareholders' agreement. CBC and Jaridah have separate divisional boards. The Executive Chairman arrangement is described in the profile as a departure from the NCCG 2018 preference; do not turn that description into an unsupported claim of full compliance.

Editorial safeguards: freedom from owner interference; no political party or candidate endorsements; separation of advertising and editorial decisions; minimum investigative and verification budgets; a full Board Editorial Committee. These are present principles, not a claim that every historical period used the same rules.

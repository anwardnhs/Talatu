# Homepage reach and imagery

## Fictional reach figures

The project owner explicitly authorized invented audience/subscriber figures on 27 October 2026. The homepage uses 85m+ monthly audience (television and digital), 8m+ monthly readers and 4m+ Odyssey+ subscribers. These are fictional character-development figures, kept in `data/fictional-impact-metrics.js`; they are not supplied profile facts or externally verified measurements. The established date remains the canonical 1962.

The lower About section uses the supplied Talatu Tower waterfront image, optimised to `public/images/talatu-tower-daylight.webp`. The About hero and Southstone news article use `public/images/southstone-campus-daylight.webp`. Careers uses the project placeholder `careers@talatu.com` rather than a separate recruitment page.
